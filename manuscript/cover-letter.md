# Draft cover letter

**To:** Editors-in-Chief, *Phytochemistry Reviews*

**Re:** Review Article — “*Artemisia* terpene diversity across scales: biosynthetic evolution and bounded antiparasitic evidence”

Dear Editors,

We submit this review for consideration in *Phytochemistry Reviews*. The manuscript critically synthesizes *Artemisia* monoterpenes, sesquiterpenes, diterpenes, triterpenes, and sesquiterpene lactones across compound identity, biosynthetic pathway, tissue and environmental context, comparative evolution, antiparasitic phenotype, parasite-target evidence, host control, and translational boundaries. Malaria and artemisinin are treated as the best-resolved benchmark, while *Artemisia absinthium* is evaluated as a preparation-specific vermifuge hypothesis rather than a proof of thujone- or lactone-specific efficacy.

The review’s contribution is an auditable evidence architecture rather than another compound catalogue. The Terpedia package links 541 registered sources, 556 source-linked evidence records, 132 antiparasitic records, 19 parasite–protein interaction records, 179 compound/specimen records, 410 audited claims, structured screening outputs, comparative-genomics diagnostics, and reproducible checksums. The manuscript distinguishes isolated compounds, mixtures, pathway candidates, phenotypes, and target hypotheses and identifies experiments needed to resolve the remaining causal gaps.
